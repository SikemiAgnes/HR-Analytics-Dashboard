# HR-Analytics-Dashboard
This HR Analytics Dashboard provides a complete view of employee distribution, attrition trends, income analysis, and workforce demographics. It helps HR teams make data-driven decisions, identify potential retention issues, and understand workforce structure across different dimensions such as job role, age, gender, education, and department.

### Dashboard Overview
The dashboard analyzes key HR metrics, showing:
- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Income distribution by job role
- Employee demographics
- Education level insights
- Department-level employee & attrition distribution
- Interactive slicers (Gender & Marital Status)

### Key Features
1. Workforce Summary Metrics
- Total Employees: 1470
- Active Employees: 1233
- Attrition Count: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.12%
2. Department Insights
- Active employees vs. attrition count by department
- Visual comparison to identify high-risk departments
3. Employee Demographics
- Age band distribution
- Gender split
- Education fields and levels
4. Income Analysis
- Average Monthly Income by Job Role
- Monthly Income for Job Roles (Active Employees)
### Tools Used

### KPIs Calculated
- Total Count of Employees
- Total Attrition Count
- Total Active Employees
- Attrition Rate
- Monthly Income for Job Roles (Active Employees)
- Average Monthly Income for Job Roles
- Active Employees by Department
- Attrition by Department
- Total Education Field by Active Employees
- Total Education Level by Active Employees
- Age Band & Gender Distribution
- Slicers for Gender & Marital Status
### Insights
- Majority of employees fall within the 25–34 and 35–44 age groups.
- Life Sciences & Medical fields dominate employee education.
- Management and Research roles have highest average income.
- R&D and Sales show notable attrition.
- Bachelor's degree holders form the largest employee group.
